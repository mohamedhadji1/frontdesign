CTI / EASM / Third Party Risk
Cyber Threat Intelligence

Monitor threats, indicators, campaigns and adversary activities relevant to your organization.

External Attack Surface

Discover exposed assets, misconfigurations and internet-facing risks before attackers exploit them.

Third-Party Assessment

Assess suppliers and partners to identify cyber risks across your extended ecosystem.

ARENA gives security leaders the visibility they need to understand their exposure, anticipate attacks and prioritize remediation. By combining intelligence, external monitoring and third-party assessment, the platform supports a proactive and risk-based cybersecurity posture.

It is built for organizations that want to go beyond reactive security and continuously monitor the threats that matter most to their business.

Keystone DNS Filtering

Keystone DNS Filtering protects users and organizations at the DNS layer by blocking malicious, unwanted or risky domains before connections are established. The solution can be deployed locally or in the cloud and combines blocklist-based filtering with AI-driven analysis.

DNS Security / AI Analysis
Local or Cloud Deployment

Flexible architecture adapted to enterprise, ISP, government or distributed environments.

Blocklist Filtering

Block access to malicious, phishing, malware, C2, suspicious or unauthorized domains.

AI-Based Analysis

Analyze DNS patterns and suspicious domains to enhance detection and prevention capabilities.

Marketing positioning

DNS is often the first step in a cyberattack. Keystone DNS Filtering turns this critical control point into a powerful protection layer, helping organizations prevent access to dangerous destinations and reduce the risk of phishing, malware infection and command-and-control communication.

With cloud and on-premise deployment options, the solution can support both centralized and sensitive environments where control, visibility and sovereignty are key requirements.

What we offer

  • DNS request filtering based on curated blocklists
  • AI-assisted detection of suspicious domain behavior
  • Protection against phishing, malware and C2 domains
  • Cloud-based or local deployment models
  • Policy-based filtering by user, group or network
  • Dashboards, reporting and security event visibility

Keystone DLP

Keystone DLP helps organizations protect sensitive data against leakage, unauthorized sharing and uncontrolled movement. The solution supports the implementation of data protection controls to detect, monitor and prevent risky data flows across the organization.

Data Loss Prevention
Sensitive Data Protection

Identify and protect confidential, personal, financial, strategic or regulated information.

Leakage Prevention

Detect and reduce data exfiltration risks through monitored channels and user actions.

Policy Enforcement

Apply rules and controls aligned with business, compliance and data governance requirements.

Marketing positioning

Data is one of the most valuable assets of any organization. Keystone DLP gives companies the ability to protect sensitive information, reduce exposure to accidental or malicious leakage and strengthen compliance with internal and regulatory data protection requirements.

The solution is designed to help organizations gain control over where sensitive data is located, how it is used and how it can leave the organization.

What we offer

  • Monitoring of risky data movements and leakage attempts
